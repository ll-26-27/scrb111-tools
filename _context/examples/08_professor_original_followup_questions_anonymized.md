# Professor's original follow-up questions (anonymized)

*Anonymized copy: the four headings name each presentation's topic instead of the students. The questions are unchanged.*

## iPSCs and organoids (sample 4)

• What does "self-organized" mean?
• What "measurements" are taken from patients to get the best iPSC-derived organoids?
• What aspects of disease might not be recapitulated in organoids? How can organoids address multi-organ diseases, or diseases with an immune component?
• Besides being useful for screening for drugs that might work best, what other ways could organoids help personalize treatments for diseases?

## Epigenetic gene silencing (sample 3)

• What do you mean by negative, permanent effects of CRISPR editing of the DNA sequence itself (i.e., cutting the DNA)?
• How would this gene silencing or activating technology be delivered to patients?

## Tooth regeneration (sample 2)

• What does "fate" mean for a cell?
• How could they have made this video smoother?
• Where in the video sequence could they have put the "superhero" analogy for enamel that might have been more effective?
• What parts were already very effective?
• How exactly are enamel and dental pulp cells connected?

## PROTACs (sample 1)

• What features of bad proteins are ubiquitin ligases scanning for? How can we be sure they don't target "good" proteins?
• How do PROTACs get into cells, and just the cells that need them to be fixed?
