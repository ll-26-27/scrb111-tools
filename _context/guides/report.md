# Writing the overview for the report

*Working draft, November 24, 2026. This file is the professor's to rewrite: the report skill reads all of it every time.*

The overview sits at the top of `outputs/report.html`, above the videos. It is what the professor reads first, so it should tell her what the set of videos looks like as a whole and where to spend her time.

## Summary

One short paragraph: how many videos, what they present, the forms they take (drawing on paper, slides, talking to camera), and anything about the set as a whole that a person watching all of them would notice.

## What comes up more than once (`themes`)

Three to six. Each is something that appears in two or more videos, said specifically enough to act on:

- a concept several students explain the same loose way, or get stuck on at the same point
- a figure or result that different students read differently
- a step of the paper that most videos skip
- a kind of visual choice that works across several videos

Name the videos under each one. A theme with one video is an observation and belongs on that video's page.

## To raise with the whole class (`for_class`)

Two to five questions or points that several videos lead to, phrased as they could be put to the room. These come from the themes; they aren't a second list of individual follow-ups.

## Keep to

- Description, not ranking. No best and worst, no scores.
- What the transcripts and stills support. If a theme rests on a term the transcriber may have misheard, say so.
- Videos named by their file name, not by the student's name, unless the professor asks otherwise.
